Schrödy Lab

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

Schrödy Lab does not collect any personal data. The app has no servers, no analytics, and no third-party components. Nothing you create in it is transmitted anywhere.

What we collect

Nothing. Schrödy Lab does not gather, transmit, store or sell personal information. We do not ask for an account, an email address or a name, and there is no way for the app to send us information about you, because the app makes no network connections of its own.

What stays on your device

Everything you make. Specifically:

  • Saved circuits. Projects you save are written to the app's private storage area on your device, as ordinary files. They are readable only by Schrödy Lab.
  • Display preferences. Small settings such as your chosen appearance are stored locally so the app opens the way you left it.

If you have iCloud Backup enabled, these files may be included in your device backup — that is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy, and we have no access to it. Deleting the app removes this data from your device.

Subscriptions

Schrödy Lab Pro is sold as an auto-renewing subscription through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes the payment, not us. We never see your name, your email address, your payment method or your billing details.

The app asks Apple's system only one question — whether this device currently has an active subscription — and receives only a yes or no. That answer is used to unlock features and is never stored by us or shared with anyone. Your purchase is governed by Apple's terms and privacy policy.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No third-party SDKs, frameworks or libraries
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking
  • No access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos
  • No user accounts and no cloud sync operated by us

Children

Schrödy Lab is suitable for all ages and is rated 4+. Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children. We do not knowingly gather personal information from any user, of any age.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export or delete the personal data a company holds about you. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export or erase. Your circuits are yours, on your device, and you can delete them at any time from within the app or by removing the app.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. If a future release of the app were to begin collecting any data, that change would be described here and reflected in the app's App Store privacy label before the release ships.
