Schrödy Lab

Quantum circuits, by hand.

Drag gates onto a circuit, watch the state vector evolve amplitude by amplitude, and export the whole thing as runnable Cirq or Qiskit code. Built for iPhone.

What it does

  • Build visually. Drag H, X, Y, Z, S, T, rotation gates, CNOT, CZ and Toffoli onto a circuit grid.
  • Simulate exactly. A full state-vector simulator computes every amplitude — probabilities and phases, not just measurement counts.
  • Step through it. Advance one column at a time and watch superposition and interference build up.
  • Export real code. Every circuit becomes runnable cirq or qiskit Python, ready to copy out.
  • Start from a preset. Bell, GHZ, uniform superposition and Grover's search are built in.
